Steel

Faster, more accurate connection design.

Design and check steel connections for the details your projects demand—from bolts and welds to base plates, frames, anchoring, and shear transfer.

  • Reusable templates for simple connections, bulk checks and parametric design
  • Visual and parametric modeling for moderate to complex joints
  • Code-checked reports ready for approval
  • A Connection Library of real-world joints to adapt and reuse

Concrete

Solve walls and D-regions.

Model concrete walls and D-regions around real geometry and loads, then review reinforcement and code checks with a clear view of structural behavior.

  • Openings, dapped ends and irregular geometry
  • Optimized reinforcement layout and material use
  • Full ULS and SLS checks in one report
  • Analysis data imported directly from SAP2000 and ETABS
