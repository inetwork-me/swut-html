This Privacy Policy explains how SWUT Solutions (“SWUT”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through swutsolutions.com, our enquiry and trial forms, events, training, and technical support.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, job title, company, business email, phone number, the products you are interested in, and the content of your messages.

  • Enquiry, quote, demo, and trial requests
  • Event and workshop registrations
  • Technical support and maintenance requests
  • Basic usage data, such as pages visited and device type

2. How we use information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange trials and demos, deliver training and support, manage software licenses, and share relevant updates about products and events. We do not sell personal information.

3. Sharing with vendors

As an authorized reseller, we may share the details needed to issue licenses, trials, or vendor-led training with our software partners, including IDEA StatiCa, ZWSOFT, MIDAS, and Bentley Systems. Each vendor processes this information under its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to function and may use analytics cookies to understand how pages are used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, including license administration and legal or accounting obligations.

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard the data entrusted to us.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@swutsolutions.com.
